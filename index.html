About

What I do

I lead engineering teams at enterprise SaaS companies — currently as a Senior Engineering Manager at iCIMS, where our teams ship the workflow automation and interview management features recruiters rely on every day. Before moving into management, I spent years as a hands-on engineer building event-driven, AWS-based systems for Fortune 100 clients, which is probably why I still care as much about the architecture as I do about the people running it.

My focus doesn't change much from role to role: connect engineering work to what the business actually needs, keep production systems reliable, and build teams where people are set up to do their best work and grow into their next one. On any given week I might be in a design review, a production incident, or a 1:1 about someone's career path — and I try to bring the same level of care to all three.

I hold a B.S. in Computer Science & Mathematics and an MBA in Supply Chain Management, both from Rutgers. Outside of work, I still write code for fun — lately that's meant small React projects and reading up on REST API design.
